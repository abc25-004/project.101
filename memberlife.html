Life As A Member

Being part of a girl group involves creativity, teamwork, discipline, and constant growth. Members experience training, performances, content creation, and unforgettable moments together.

DANCE PRACTICE

Dance practice is one of the most important parts of training as a group. Members spend hours learning choreography, improving synchronization, and developing stage presence.

VOCAL LESSONS

Vocal training helps members improve technique, confidence, harmony, and control while singing. Lessons focus on strengthening vocals and learning how to perform live confidently.

SONGWRITING

Songwriting sessions allow members to express creativity, emotions, and ideas through music. Writing original songs helps create a unique identity for the group.

TEAM DISCUSSIONS

Team discussions are important for communication, planning, and problem-solving within the group. Members learn how to share opinions respectfully and work together toward common goals.

CONTENT CREATION

Content creation includes filming videos, taking photos, interacting with fans, and building the group’s image online. Creative content allows members to connect with supporters while showing their personalities.
