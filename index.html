Welcome To Project 101

Congratulations on becoming part of Project 101. This website is designed to act as your introduction and guide into the world of this group. Here, you will discover the importance of teamwork, creativity, dedication, communication, and growth while gaining insight into the experiences and responsibilities that come with being part of a girl group.

Throughout this journey, members will experience dance practices, vocal lessons, performances, content creation, songwriting, and unforgettable memories together. We hope every member continues to work hard, support one another, and grow both individually and as a team while building something meaningful together.
